# Third-party assets

## Material Design Icons

`res/drawable/mdi_*.xml` are icons from [Material Design Icons](https://pictogrammers.com/library/mdi/)
by Pictogrammers (`@mdi/svg` 7.4.47), converted to Android vector drawables (path data unchanged,
fill colour set). Licensed under the Apache License 2.0:
https://github.com/Templarian/MaterialDesign/blob/master/LICENSE

Brand icons (Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo Switch) depict trademarks of their owners; they are used
only to label the matching HDMI input and imply no endorsement.
